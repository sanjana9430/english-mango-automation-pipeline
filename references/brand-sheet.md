Built from three sources: (1) the existing englishmango.com site, read directly from its code, (2) 7 reference reels in the luxury-stay/staycation space, reviewed on-page (no video files downloaded — see note at bottom), and (3) 4 reference carousel posts in the same niche, added 2026-11-15 when carousel days were scheduled but the sheet only covered Reels (see section 4).

**Pattern:** on-screen text splits roughly into three camps — a white card/pill with bold black text (3 of 7), plain white text directly on the footage with no card (2 of 7), or no text at all (2 of 7). The single best-performing reel in this set used the simplest option: two short lines of plain white text, no card, over a moody, slow, golden/blue-hour shot. Minimal restraint reads as more premium than a busy card here — which matches the "Apple-style" brief.

**Shared caption-copy pattern** (the paragraph under the reel, not the on-screen text): property name + host tag, a short bullet list of amenities, a location pin, and a save/share prompt at the end. Several lean on a numbered or bulleted list rather than flowing prose.

**Shared visual grading:** golden hour or blue hour, heavy greenery, pools, slow walking or push-in camera moves — no fast whip-pans, no meme-style quick cuts. Audio skews toward moody indie/lo-fi or romantic instrumental mashups rather than trending meme sounds.

## 3. Merged direction for English Mango reels

Reconciling the two — where the reel genre's best practice and the site's existing brand agree, and where they don't:

- **On-screen text:** follow the top performer's lead — plain text, no busy card, 1–2 short lines max per beat. But use the site's actual palette instead of generic white: **cream (`#FAF8F5`) text with a soft forest-green (`#1F2E26`) drop shadow**, or invert to pine-green text on a barely-there cream scrim for brighter shots.
- **Typography conflict to flag:** the site's display font, Playfair Display, is a serif — elegant for a static heading, but serifs can get mushy at small size during fast motion. Recommendation: reserve Playfair Display for the opening title card and the end card only (where the frame holds still); use Inter (the site's own body font) for every in-motion lower-third caption. This keeps it on-brand without sacrificing legibility.
- **Color accents:** use the amber/gold primary (`#9F5E09`) exactly the way the reference set uses its coral accent card — sparingly, for one punch moment (a stat, a price, a CTA), not for every caption.
- **Pacing:** slow push-ins and walk-toward/away-from-camera moves, golden hour or blue hour grading, negative space held for a beat before the next cut — no whip-pans, no meme-speed jump cuts.
- **Caption copy (the paragraph, not on-screen text):** property feature → short bullet list of what's included (pool, suites, temple, theatre, lift) → location line → a save/share prompt. This matches both the reel genre's convention and englishmango.com's own feature-list structure on the site.
- **Music:** moody instrumental or lo-fi, not trending meme audio — consistent with both the reel set and the "Apple-style, premium" brief.

## 4. What the reference carousels do

Reels and carousels are different genres on this platform, not just a format swap — a carousel's first slide has to earn a swipe, and only the first slide reliably gets seen. Reviewed 4 real carousel posts in the same India luxury-villa/staycation niche as the reels above (data pulled via Instagram's own post metadata and slide images, not downloaded media — same no-download principle as the reel research; one additional @explorishh carousel, an awards-ceremony post, turned up in the same data pull but was excluded as off-niche):

| Creator | Post | Slides | Cover-slide treatment | Inner-slide treatment |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| @travel_pods_ | Mudhouse retreat near Delhi-NCR | 10 | 4-photo grid collage + bold yellow marker-style hook ("This is not Bali..") + plain white location subtext, both centered on the grid's dividing line | Same 4-photo-grid layout repeated, one plain white one-line caption per amenity ("Mud Cottages with cozy room & rustic charm," "Swimming pool made for lazy afternoons") |
| @explorishh | "4 Newly Launched Stays Near Nashik" | 5 | Single full-bleed hero photo; small sans-serif intro line ("4 Newly Launched Stays Near") + large serif proper-noun anchor ("NASHIK"), top-left; subtle creator tag bottom-left; ">>" swipe-prompt bottom-right | One villa per slide (this post is a multi-property roundup, not a single-property amenity tour) |
| @travelwithkavs | Ladakh carousel | 7 | Caption reviewed, not slide images — contributes to the caption-pattern finding below, not the visual-treatment table | — |

Only the two rows above were visually inspected slide-by-slide; the third contributed caption data only.

**Two real cover-slide conventions, not one:** a punchy, colorful, meme-adjacent hook (yellow marker font, "this is not X" framing) over a busy photo grid; or a quieter single-hero-photo cover with the location name as a large serif anchor and a small swipe-prompt. The second is closer to englishmango.com's own existing typographic hierarchy (Playfair Display already *is* the site's proper-noun/heading font) — the first would be introducing a voice the brand doesn't have anywhere else.

**Caption pattern, confirmed and sharper than the reel research found:** every one of the 4 posts leans harder into bullets/emoji than the reels did — amenity lists with a leading emoji per line (🏊 🎯 🚜 🍽️ 🌄), a location pin, and an explicit "Save & Share this post" or "Save this for your next trip" line near the top or bottom of the caption, not just at the end.

**Slide count:** 5, 7, 8, and 10 across the 4 posts reviewed — `content_calendar.yaml`'s `min_slides: 3` / `max_slides: 10` for `luxury_carousel` already covers this range, no change needed there.

## 5. Merged direction for English Mango carousels

English Mango is one villa, not a multi-property roundup — so the "4 Newly Launched Stays" listicle structure doesn't map directly; it's closer in spirit to `@travel_pods_`'s single-property amenity tour (one feature per slide), just not its busy 4-photo-grid-per-slide execution.

- **Cover slide:** single full-bleed photo (the Exterior Hero or a comparable wide shot), not a 4-photo grid — grids read as busier/more listicle-y than this brand's existing calm, uncluttered site. Large **Playfair Display** proper-noun anchor ("HARIDWAR" or "ENGLISH MANGO") plus a short **Inter** intro line above it, same font pairing already established for reels' title/end cards in section 3. Small swipe-prompt in the corner (the reference set's `>>` convention, or Instagram's own carousel dots).
- **Inner slides:** one feature per slide, single photo (not a grid — a grid only makes sense when a slide covers multiple sub-areas of one amenity, which these aren't), with the same **plain cream-text-on-forest-shadow caption treatment** already defined in `brand-tokens.json`'s `caption_style` for reels — reused here for consistency across formats, rather than adopting the reference set's plain-white-no-shadow convention.
- **Caption copy:** sharpen section 3's caption guidance with what the carousel set confirmed more strongly — lead with a "Save this for your next trip" or "Save & share" line near the top (not just the end), then an emoji-led bullet per amenity (🏊 pool, 🛕 private marble sanctum, 🎬 home theatre, 🏋️ gym), then the location line. More emoji-forward than the reel captions, matching what this specific sub-genre (carousel, not reel) actually does.
- **Last slide:** CTA/end-card, same convention as the reel's CTA beat — property name + englishmango.com, Playfair Display, held static (a carousel's last slide is naturally "held" the way a reel's end card is).

## 6. Actual @englishmangoluxury posts — the target standard (2026-11-19)

Unlike sections 2–5 (other creators' reels/carousels in the same niche, used to infer direction), these two are **the brand's own published posts**, specified directly as *"this is how it should be... this is how reels and posts and carousels should look like."* This section takes priority over 2–5 wherever they conflict.

**Reel** (`Df4I-EZSfRo`, "Kumbh 2027 starts 14 Jan. Do it the calm way."): opening frame is the deep forest-green (`#1F2E26`) background, small tracked-out all-caps amber/gold (`#9F5E09`) label top-left reading "KUMBH 2027 · HARIDWAR", small cream `@englishmangoluxury` watermark bottom-left. Extremely restrained — large areas of flat dark negative space, minimal text, no card/box behind it. This matches section 3's "minimal restraint reads as more premium" finding closely.

**Carousel** (`Df2SS8okjRG`, "Most people try to do Haridwar in one day...", 10 slides) — this is the more significant find; it's noticeably more structured than what sections 4–5 described or what the current render engine (`remotion/src/beats/Caption.tsx` etc.) produces:
- **Alternating slide backgrounds**: dark forest-green slides (cover, and others) alternate with warm cream/beige (`#EBE7E0`-toned) slides — not every slide shares one background.
- **Consistent small amber eyebrow label**, top-left, same wording across all slides in a post ("HARIDWAR, SLOWLY") — a running series title, distinct from the per-slide headline.
- **Large serif (Playfair Display) headline** per slide — the actual hook/point of that slide, e.g. "Think in areas, not checklists." / "Arrive & slow down" — not a single plain caption line.
- **Body paragraph** below the headline in regular-weight sans (Inter), 2–3 sentences, actual prose rather than a bullet fragment.
- **A highlighted callout/tip box** near the bottom of several slides — a lighter rounded rectangle (tan on the green slides, slightly darker cream on the cream slides) containing one bolded-partial-phrase fact or tip, e.g. *"English Mango sits on the Haridwar Highway, about **20 minutes (15 km)** from Har Ki Pauri — close enough for darshan, far enough for sleep."* / *"**Villa tip:** Don't plan sightseeing on arrival day..."*
- **Day-by-day slides use a labeled timeline list**: small amber time-of-day labels (AFTERNOON / SUNSET / NIGHT) each paired with one short line, under a thin horizontal divider below the slide's headline.
- Consistent bottom-left `@englishmangoluxury` watermark on every slide, same as the reel.

**Gap to flag honestly**: the current Remotion render engine (`remotion/src/beats/`) does not yet produce this — it renders one short plain caption per beat over a Ken-Burns photo, no eyebrow label, no alternating background slides, no callout boxes, no timeline-list layout. Matching this reference properly means extending the beat components (new props for eyebrow text, callout-box content, optional timeline items), not just a copy/prompt tweak in `ideate.py`. Flagging this as a real gap rather than quietly building toward it — worth a separate, deliberate pass when you're ready, not bundled into today's bug-fixing.

## 7. Color-grading reference — two Rome reels (not villa content)

`DTh9VnJjMjR` and `DYlQPhMRJHB` (Terrazza delle Quadrighe, Rome) are off-niche for subject matter — explicitly referenced for **lighting/color only**, not composition or text style:
- `DTh9VnJjMjR`: golden-hour backlight through tall stone columns, warm amber/orange sky glow, silhouetted figures, soft sun flare — the warmer of the two.
- `DYlQPhMRJHB`: same column-framing technique in bright daylight — cool blue sky, hazy warm-toned skyline in the distance, small plain white drop-shadowed caption centered ("My favorite hidden gem in Rome").

Shared technique worth noting for shot selection/grading (not for the villa's own architecture, which is a different style of building): strong **architectural-element framing** — shooting through columns/arches toward a view, rather than a plain wide establishing shot — plus a preference for natural warm light over flat/neutral grading. Relevant to shot composition and color grading only; not a layout or text-treatment reference.
